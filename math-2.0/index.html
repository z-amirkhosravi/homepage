    Research#

    • Lattice Eisenstein series and representations: The nonspherical realization of quaternionic lattice coefficients and harmonic transitions.
    • Harmonic theta series and special L-values: Mellin normalization and an explicit quaternionic coefficient expressed through a weight-six modular L-value.
    • Regular automorphic forms and cohomology: The flat-coefficient two-form, positive pairing and period map.
    • Constructing regular automorphic forms: The constant-spinor Poincaré construction and explicit nonvanishing estimates.
    • Cusp Forms, Mellin Transforms and Parabolic Cohomology: The incomplete-Mellin primitive, function-valued period cocycle and surface periods.
    • Similitude Hecke operators and integral eigenvalues: Compatible normalizations and an integral coefficient lattice.
    • Automorphic cohomology and geometric realization: The explicit real family of polarized abelian fourfolds and its Hecke isogenies.

    How to read this wiki#

    Start with the foundations, or go directly to the construction if you know quaternions and real differentiation. Each article states its conventions and links to the mathematics it uses.

    A supplied proof, an independent review, and a formal verification are different kinds of evidence. The articles contain explicit arguments and identify published theorems used as inputs; review status and supporting evidence are attached to individual results. The cusp restriction exact sequence has received a separate AI review. No result in this published collection is labeled Lean-verified. See About this wiki.

    Follow the links between constructions and results to move through the subject. Open questions remain visibly separate from the statements with supplied proofs.
