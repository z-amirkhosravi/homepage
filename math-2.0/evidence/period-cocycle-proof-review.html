    Review of the Period-Cocycle Proofs

    An independent AI reviewer examined three revised proofs in Explicit Regular Cusp Forms and Period Cocycles for Sp(1,1) on 1 November 2026. The review concerns the incomplete-Mellin primitive, triangle-period relations and function-valued period cocycle, each in formulation version 1. Their statements are unchanged. The reviewer found no blocking defect in these proof corrections within the scope described below.

    The Incomplete-Mellin Primitive#

    For the incomplete-Mellin primitive, the review checked the exponential majorant, termwise integration and differentiation, dependence on the Mellin parameter, the primitive's sign, and decay of its derivatives. The Fourier expansion and coefficient estimates remain stated inputs.

    Triangle Periods and Their Relations#

    For triangle periods and their relations, the review checked that the smooth lift satisfies the hypotheses of the cited Miller–Schmid results and that the finite decomposition supplies smooth vectors in cuspidal square-integrable representations. It also checked derivative decay, transport to the setting of the arithmetic cuspidal injection and the period signs. That injection and its preceding comparison results remain imported dependencies; their full proofs were not re-audited.

    The Function-Valued Period Cocycle#

    For the function-valued period cocycle, the review checked the group action, normalization constants, connecting-map signs, primitive changes modulo constants, real analyticity, and transport to each full cusp stabilizer. It checked the degree-one restriction coboundary directly, including finite holonomy and both odd and even integer weights in the stated range, with the lowest weight included.

    Scope and Fixed Revision#

    The reviewer read the account before consulting the author's evidence, inspected the identified Miller–Schmid passages, and replayed the retained exact-algebra check. That finite calculation supports the sign and normalization checks; the analytic arguments were assessed separately. The fourteen unchanged proofs, general cusp and cohomology theories, and proofs of imported literature theorems are outside this review. No Lean verification is claimed for these three results.
