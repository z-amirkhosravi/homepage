    Review of Parabolic Cohomology and Compact Support

    The mathematical baseline of the article received an independent AI review on 18 October 2026. A separate reviewing agent first read the exposition without the author's research notes, then checked the argument and the cited source passages.     Reviewed Content#

    The review covers the definitions of interior cohomology and parabolic cohomology, and the cusp restriction exact sequence, each in formulation version 1. It includes both presentations of the proof, the torus-cusp example, and the conditional application to automorphic cohomology.

    The checks addressed the group and flat-bundle coefficient conventions; compatibility of subgroup restriction with restriction to cusp cross-sections; the relative and compact-support comparison in both directions; the connecting map; and the cutoff construction. The concise and expanded proofs were checked to follow the same argument.

    Sources and Scope#

    The reviewer inspected the cited passages in Brown's Cohomology of Groups, Bott–Tu's Differential Forms in Algebraic Topology, and Martelli's revised An Introduction to Geometric Topology, version 4, September 2025. The article's bibliography gives the precise locations. The extension of the constant-coefficient comparison to flat coefficients is supplied in the article and was included in the review.

    The finite-volume cusp theorem, existence of good covers, and small-chain comparison are imported foundational results. The review did not audit every theorem on which these sources depend. It concerns the topological comparison with interior cohomology; an identification with cuspidal automorphic cohomology would require additional spectral results.
