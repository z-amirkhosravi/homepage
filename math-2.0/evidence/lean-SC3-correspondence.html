    Formal Correspondence for the Spinor–Fueter Lift

    The SC3 formalization uses three checked statements from cumulative Lean revision ffb57ce0878663717056b44ebb7ac77ead6c2195:

    • SC3_v1 gives the weighted differential identity and equivalence of the lifted group kernel with the ball Fueter equation.
    • SC3_smooth_correspondence_v1 gives smoothness and the mutually inverse lift and section formulas on their stated domains.
    • SC3_ball_covariance_v1 proves preservation of the Fueter kernel by the ball action for degrees at least one, under the displayed differentiability hypothesis.

    Smooth Representatives and Domains#

    Group functions are represented by functions on the ambient quaternion matrix space. The smooth correspondence requires ContDiffAt at points satisfying IsSp11; its inverse identities are restricted to the group and the open ball. The formalization supplies smooth ambient representatives. It does not supply an intrinsic Lie-group ContMDiff comparison theorem or a quotient identifying all representatives that agree on the group.

    The separately checked group-derivative identities identify the right derivative with the exponential-path derivative and prove independence of the off-group representative. These are the group-restricted assertions used by the kernel argument.

    Operator Normalization#

    The factor 1/24 is the chosen normalization of the Schmid operator, corresponding to formula (L12). The formal calculation checks the differential identity with this definition; it does not independently derive the numerical factor from a construction of the Killing form.
