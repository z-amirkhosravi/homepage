    An independent AI reviewer compared the written arguments with the Lean declarations for the coefficient rotation action, the spinor connection and Fueter correspondence, and the discrete-series realization. The review concerns EI7, SC1–SC3 and DS1–DS4, each in formulation version 1. Compilation and the declaration-level axiom audit are recorded separately in the build log.

    Arguments and Background Results#

    The comparison checks the actual argument, including multiplication order, polynomial degree, group action, weights, analytic hypotheses and norm constants.     Formal Scope#

    DS2–DS4 cover degrees at least two. The background mean-value results concern the quaternionic unit ball and complete real normed targets; the radial formula includes its weighted integrability hypothesis. The invariant-measure interface is specialized to the stated group and normalization. The compact representation results concern binary forms.

The check establishes the stated group-restricted identities; an intrinsic Lie-group manifold comparison and a derivation of the normalization from the Killing form are outside its scope.
