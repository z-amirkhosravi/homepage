    Research

    The maximal-parabolic Eisenstein realization, its irreducible compact-type ladder, and rotation covariance.

    Lattice Eisenstein series and representations

    Discussion

    Varying a four-dimensional lattice turns the elliptic coefficients into automorphic functions on a special linear group. We identify both their inducing data and the irreducible module they generate. This is a different group action from the Möbius action on the ball.

    Harmonic numerators of the kernel derivatives#

    Lemma EI1. Harmonic numerators in every derivative order

    For ν∈ℕ03, r=|ν|, and N(q)=|q|2, there is a quaternion-valued homogeneous polynomial Hν of degree r+1, with harmonic real components, such that

    Gν(q)=Hν(q)N(q)r+2.

    It obeys H0=q‾ and Hν+ej=N∂jHν−2(r+2)xjHν.

    EI1 · version 1

    Uses Kernel, multi-indices.

    Proof of Lemma EI1

    Start with H0=q‾. Differentiation gives the recurrence

    Hν+ej=N∂jHν−2(r+2)xjHν.(E3)

    It is polynomial and has degree r+2. If Hν is harmonic of degree r+1, then

    Δ(N∂jHν)=(4r+8)∂jHν,Δ(xjHν)=2∂jHν,

    so (E3) is harmonic. This proves the lemma for every order.

    Discussion

    The lattice sums Eν(L) and their relation to derivatives of the regular part of ℘L,j are established in N1–N3. They converge for r>1, and even r gives zero. We therefore focus on odd r≥3. Ordinary Taylor coefficients include the multi-index factorials.

    Homogeneous sections and Eisenstein summation#

    Definition EI-D1. The homogeneous induced representation

    Set G4=SL4(ℝ), Γ=SL4(ℤ), K4=SO(4). Use row vectors and Lg=ℤ4g. Let w=r+3>4, and let Iw be the smooth even complex functions on ℝ4∖{0} homogeneous of degree −w:

    f(tx)=|t|−wf(x),(R(g)f)(x)=f(xg).

    The same definition is valid for any real w>4, without requiring a derivative origin. Let e4=(0,0,0,1) and

    P={(Ab0d):det⁡(A)d=1},Ff(g)=f(e4g).

    Write 𝒴l for complex harmonic homogeneous polynomials of degree l restricted to S3; Vl=Syml⁡ℂ2.

    EI-D1 · version 1

    Construction EI-C1. The lattice Eisenstein function

    For w>4 and f∈Iw, form

    ℰf(g)=∑m∈ℤ4∖{0}f(mg).

    EI-C1 · version 1

    Well-definedness: Convergence, smoothness, and equivariance in EI2.

    Theorem EI2. A maximal-parabolic Eisenstein series cf. [2, §§2.4–2.5, Lemma 2.9]

    For w>4, ℰf is smooth, left Γ-invariant, of moderate growth with all right derivatives, and satisfies ℰR(h)f(g)=ℰf(gh). Moreover

    ℰf(g)=2ζ(w)∑γ∈(P∩Γ)\ΓFf(γg).

    The section law is Ff(pg)=|d|−wFf(g). In normalized left-equivariant/right-translation induction, with δP(p)=|d|−4, the inducing character is χ(p)=|d|2−w. For an odd derivative order r≥3, the scalar components of Gν give w=r+3 and χ=|d|−(r+1).

    EI2 · version 1

    Uses EI-D1, EI-C1.

    Proof of Theorem EI2cf. [2, Lemma 2.9]

    The sum and all its g-derivatives converge uniformly on compact subsets of G4: differentiation preserves degree −w, and |mg|≫C|m| on a compact set C. Hence ℰf is smooth, left Γ-invariant, and

    ℰR(h)f(g)=ℰf(gh).(E6)

    Each scalar component of Gν is such an f, so EI-C1 includes the original coefficient sums. The bound |ℰf(g)|≤Cf‖g−1‖w∑m≠0|m|−w, and the same bound for right derivatives, gives moderate growth.

    Let P be the maximal parabolic consisting of matrices

    p=(Ab0d),det⁡(A)d=1,

    which stabilizes the row line ℝe4. Put Ff(g)=f(e4g). Then

    Ff(pg)=|d|−wFf(g),δP(p)=|d|−4.(E7)

    Every primitive integral row occurs as e4γ, and the stabilizer of the primitive line is P∩Γ. Grouping a nonzero row by its positive gcd and then identifying opposite primitive rows gives

    ℰf(g)=2ζ(w)∑γ∈(P∩Γ)\ΓFf(γg).(E8)

    This is a maximal-parabolic Eisenstein series in its region of absolute convergence. In the left-equivariant/right-translation convention F(pg)=δP(p)1/2χ(p)F(g), its normalized inducing character is

    χ(p)=|d|2−w=|d|−(r+1).(E9)

    Specifying the section law removes any ambiguity from authors who use the inverse character or opposite parabolic.

    The irreducible module selected by the coefficients#

    Definition EI-D2. The vanishing-moment subspace

    Let w≥6 be even, d=w−4, and let 𝒫d be the homogeneous degree-d polynomials on ℝ4, with action p(x)↦p(xg). Define

    ℳw={f∈Iw:∫S3f(ω)p(ω)dσ(ω)=0 for every p∈𝒫d}.(E10)

    EI-D2 · version 1

    Proposition EI3. Moment kernel and finite-dimensional quotient

    For even w≥6, the moment map gives an equivariant exact sequence

    0→ℳw→Iw→𝒫w−4∨→0.

    Its compact-finite kernel is ⨁j≥0𝒴w−2+2j. Every scalar derivative kernel of order r=w−3 lies in its minimal compact type 𝒴w−2.

    EI3 · version 1

    Uses EI-D2, EI1.

    Proof of Proposition EI3

    The pairing is bilinear, not Hermitian. Since the product has degree −4, angular change of variables under g∈SL4 shows

    ∫S3f(ωg)p(ωg)dσ(ω)=∫S3f(ω)p(ω)dσ(ω).

    Indeed for η=ωg/|ωg|, dσ(η)=|ωg|−4dσ(ω). Thus (E10) is invariant, and the pairing induces an exact sequence

    0⟶ℳw⟶Iw⟶𝒫w−4∨⟶0.(E11)

    Surjectivity follows from the nondegenerate spherical pairing on the finite-dimensional polynomial space. Harmonic decomposition of 𝒫w−4 gives

    (ℳw)K4=⨁j≥0𝒴w−2+2j,(E12)

    where the subscript means K4-finite vectors. The low harmonics 𝒴0,𝒴2,…,𝒴w−4 constitute the finite-dimensional quotient. By EI1, every scalar Fueter kernel derivative of order r=w−3 belongs to the minimal compact type 𝒴w−2 of this kernel.

    Theorem EI4. Irreducibility and harmonic transitions cf. [1]

    The (𝔰𝔩4,SO(4))-module (ℳw)K4 is irreducible for every even w≥6. Its compact types are

    ⨁j≥0𝒴w−2+2j≃⨁j≥0Vw−2+2j⊠Vw−2+2j.

    EI4 · version 1

    Uses EI3, irreducibility of spherical harmonics under SO(4).

    Proof of Theorem EI4cf. [1]

    For a symmetric trace-free real matrix A, write

    QA(x)=xAxt,DAH=(xA)⋅∇H,TAH=∑ijAij∂i∂jH.

    Represent a compact type H∈𝒴l by fH(x)=|x|−w−lH(x). On S3 the infinitesimal action is

    dR(A)H=DAH−(w+l)QAH.(E13)

    It has harmonic degrees l+2,l,l−2. Its projection onto degree l−2 is exactly

    prl−2⁡dR(A)H=l+2−w4l(l+1)TAH.(E14)

    To see this, ΔDAH=2TAH and Δ2(QAH)=8TAH. Harmonic decomposition therefore gives degree-l−2 terms TAH/(2l) in DAH|S3 and TAH/[4l(l+1)] in (QAH)|S3, yielding (E14). The degree-l+2 term is −(w+l) times the top harmonic part of QAH.

    The lowering map vanishes precisely at l=w−2 and is nonzero at every subsequent degree in (E12); the raising map is nonzero at every degree. Nonzeroness can be seen over the complexified Lie algebra by taking H=(x1+ix2)l and quadratic forms (x1±ix2)2: the plus choice raises harmonically, the minus choice gives a nonzero TAH when l≥2. Each 𝒴l is an irreducible K4-module of multiplicity one. Any nonzero submodule contains a compact type; successive nonzero lowering and raising maps then give all of (E12). This proves irreducibility. The identification 𝒴l≃Vl⊠Vl follows from the usual Spin⁡(4) harmonic-polynomial representation; (−1,−1) acts trivially.

    Nonvanishing and the automorphic realization#

    Theorem EI5. The coefficient-generated automorphic module

    For odd r≥3, the scalar components of g↦Eν(ℤ4g), |ν|=r, generate an automorphic (𝔰𝔩4,SO(4))-module isomorphic to (ℳr+3)K4. The Eisenstein map is injective on this module. Its minimal compact type is 𝒴r+1.

    EI5 · version 1

    Uses EI1, EI2, EI4.

    Proof of Theorem EI5

    An intertwining sum could conceivably vanish on the entire module, so this needs a proof. Choose H∈𝒴w−2 with H(e4)≠0, and put

    ga=diag⁡(a,a,a,a−3),a→∞,fH(x)=|x|−2w+2H(x).

    The rows (0,0,0,k) contribute exactly 2ζ(w)a3wH(e4). All other rows satisfy |fH(x)|≤C|x|−w. For m′≠0,

    ∑k∈ℤ(a2|m′|2+a−6k2)−w/2≪a−w|m′|−w+a4−w|m′|1−w.

    Both sums over m′∈ℤ3∖0 converge for w>4. Consequently

    ℰfH(ga)=2ζ(w)a3wH(e4)+O(a4−w).(E15)

    The Eisenstein map is nonzero on the minimal type, hence injective on the irreducible module (E12). The scalar components of the order-r kernel derivatives contain a nonzero element of that type: for example ∂xrG(ix)=−i(−1)r(3)rx−r−3 for x>0. Its compact orbit spans the type and its enveloping-algebra orbit spans (E12).

    Discussion

    The fixed-order coefficients occupy the minimal compact type. General SL4 transformations generate the larger space (E12); they do not preserve the flat Fueter equation or act within the finite tuple of coefficients.

    This specifies an irreducible real-place Harish-Chandra module. It does not assert a unitary L2(Γ\G4) realization or a discrete series of SL4. The proof concerns the algebraic module of K-finite automorphic functions; a choice of globalization/topology or an adelic classification with finite-place constituents is additional work. A coefficient can vanish at a particular symmetric lattice even though the automorphic function is nonzero. For example cubic-lattice symmetries force numerous components to vanish.

    Corollary EI6. The first nonzero derivative order

    For r=3, the inducing degree is −6, the normalized character is |d|−4, the minimal compact type is V4⊠V4 of dimension 25, and the finite-dimensional quotient of I6 is 𝒫2∨ of dimension 10.

    EI6 · version 1

    Uses EI3, EI5.

    Proof of Corollary EI6

    Insert w=6 in EI3–EI5. The dimensions are (4+1)2=25 and (2+4−13)=10.

    Rotation covariance of the coefficient tuple#

    Proposition EI7. The coefficient action descends to the rotation group

    Let U−r−3 be the right-ℍ span of the order-r kernel derivatives, with r>1, and let ρ(a,b)f(q)=bf(a−1qb) for unit quaternions a,b. Set αL(f)=∑λ≠0f(λ). Then

    (σ(a,b)α)(f)=bα(ρ(a,b)−1f),αaLb−1=σ(a,b)αL.

    This is a genuine real representation on right-ℍ-linear functionals and factors through SO(4).

    EI7 · version 1

    Uses Convergent sums.

    Proof of Proposition EI7

    The identity G(a−1qb)=b−1G(q)a follows directly from the kernel formula. Differentiate it in any real coordinate directions. The chain rule expresses rotated order-r derivatives using all four real derivative directions, with constant right quaternion factors. The kernel is also right regular: direct differentiation gives ∂tG+∂xGi+∂yGj+∂zGk=0. This identity, and its derivatives, eliminate every time derivative in favor of spatial derivatives with right coefficients. Thus U−r−3 is invariant under ρ. The formula for ρ gives its group law and right-ℍ linearity. Reindexing the absolutely convergent sum gives the displayed identity. For composition, the two output factors multiply as b1b2, and the precomposition is ρ(k2−1)ρ(k1−1)=ρ((k1k2)−1). At (−1,−1), the input representation is −1, while the output also gets −1, so σ(−1,−1)α(f)=−α(−f)=α(f). The kernel of Spin⁡(4)→SO(4) therefore acts trivially.

    Discussion

    The arithmetic quotient is Γ\G4/K4; lattice stabilizers give the usual orbifold interpretation. The scalar-component model above avoids choosing local frames. The underlying induced representations are part of the theory studied by [1]; the proofs here specify our conventions directly. For the standard primitive-vector identity compare [2], §2.5. The next article establishes the theta and special-value interpretation, including the cubic example.
