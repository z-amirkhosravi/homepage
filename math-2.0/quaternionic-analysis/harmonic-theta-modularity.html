    Harmonic Theta Transformation#

    Theorem TH2. Modularity and cuspidality of the coset vector

    For an even integral positive-definite rank-four lattice and homogeneous harmonic H of even degree ℓ>0, the coset vector is a holomorphic cusp form of weight k=2+ℓ for the lattice Weil representation. Explicitly,

    Θμ(τ+1)=e2πiQ(μ)Θμ(τ),
    Θμ(−1/τ)=−τk|D|∑η∈De−2πi(μ,η)Θη(τ).

    The pairings and Q(μ) in the phases are taken modulo ℤ.

    TH2 · version 1

    Uses TH-D1, Poisson summation.

    Proof of Theorem TH2

    The series and its derivatives converge locally uniformly in the upper half-plane by Gaussian decay. Integrality gives Q(λ+μ)≡Q(μ)(modℤ) for λ∈L, proving the first transformation.

    For the Fourier transform ϕˆ(y)=∫ℝ4ϕ(x)e−2πi(x,y)dx, the harmonic Gaussian identity [5, Thm. 2] gives

    H(x)eπiτN(x)ˆ(y)=(−iτ)−2τ−ℓH(y)e−πiN(y)/τ.

    Indeed multiplication by H(x) becomes H(−(2πi)−1∂y) on the Gaussian transform. All contracted lower-degree terms contain a Laplacian of H and vanish; the highest-degree term is τ−ℓH(y). Apply this identity with −1/τ and use the shifted-lattice Poisson formula. The volume factor is vol⁡(ℝ4/L)−1=|D|−1/2, and (−i(−1/τ))−2(−1/τ)−ℓ=−τ2+ℓ. Poisson initially gives the phase with a plus sign; replacing η by −η gives the displayed sign since H is even and Θ−η=Θη.

    Each component has zero constant term: the only possible norm-zero vector is zero and H(0)=0. All other terms decay exponentially at infinity. The two transformations generate the modular group and express every cusp transform as a finite linear combination of components. They therefore prove holomorphy and vanishing at every cusp. The coset transformation laws are [5, Props. 2–3], with h/N representing the discriminant coset.

    The Newform Correspondence#

    Corollary TH6. The modular representation attached to the example

    The newform f determines a cuspidal automorphic representation of GL2(𝔸ℚ) of conductor four and trivial central character. Its real factor is the weight-six discrete series; its holomorphic component has lowest weight six on SL2(ℝ).

    TH6 · version 1

    Uses TH4, TH5, the classical newform–automorphic-representation correspondence.

    Proof of Corollary TH6

    TH4 establishes a normalized new eigenform of exact level four and trivial nebentypus. Apply adelization and the newform correspondence [2, Thm. 2.2, Prop. 2.15]: the finite conductor is the new level, the central character is the nebentypus, and the holomorphic weight specifies the archimedean discrete series. In the unitary automorphic normalization, L(f,s)=L(πf,s−5/2); hence the value here is L(πf,5/2).

    TH5 expresses the quaternionic coefficient as a critical value of its standard L-function in the classical normalization.
