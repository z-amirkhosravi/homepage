    Quaternionic Elliptic Coefficients as Lattice Sums

    The elliptic construction starts from an arbitrary full-rank lattice. Its coefficients supply a first connection to lattice Eisenstein sums. The precise statements below lead to representations and special values, followed by questions about arithmetic multiplication and periods.

    Higher derivatives and Eisenstein sums#

    For ν=(n1,n2,n3)∈ℕ03, set

    |ν|=n1+n2+n3,∂ν=∂x1n1∂x2n2∂x3n3,Gν=∂νG.

    Write ϵj for the multi-index with a single 1 in position j, for j∈{1,2,3}.

    Construction QC.1. Let L⊂ℍ be a full-rank lattice and let ν∈ℕ03 satisfy |ν|≥2. Form

    Eν(L)=∑λ∈L∗Gν(λ),℘L,ν(q)=∑λ∈LGν(q+λ)−Eν(L)(q∉L).

    Proposition QC.2. For every full-rank lattice L⊂ℍ and every ν∈ℕ03 with |ν|≥2, the Eisenstein series in Construction QC.1 converges absolutely. The defining series for ℘L,ν converges absolutely and locally uniformly on ℍ∖L. For every ω∈L and q∉L,

    ℘L,ν(q+ω)=℘L,ν(q).

    Proposition QC.2 · version 1

    Uses Construction QC.1, Lemma QF.4, the lattice-count estimate in Proposition QW.2.

    Proof of Proposition QC.2

    Lemma QF.4 gives decay |q|−3−|ν|, with exponent at least 5. The lattice-count estimate from the proof of Proposition QW.2 therefore proves absolute convergence, uniformly on compact sets away from the lattice for the translated sum. Absolute convergence permits reindexing λ↦λ+ω, which proves periodicity.

    Proposition QC.3. Let L⊂ℍ be a full-rank lattice and j∈{1,2,3}. Let hj be the smooth extension near 0 of ℘L,j−Gj supplied by Proposition QW.2. For every μ∈ℕ03 with |μ|≥1,

    ∂μhj(0)=Eμ+ϵj(L).

    Proposition QC.3 · version 2

    Uses Higher derivatives and Eisenstein sums, Construction QC.1, Proposition QC.2, Proposition QW.2.

    Proof of Proposition QC.3

    Differentiate the convergent series for hj from Proposition QW.2 and evaluate at q=0. The subtracted constants disappear, leaving ∑λ∈L∗Gμ+ϵj(λ). This converges absolutely since |μ+ϵj|≥2.

    Corollary QC.4. For every full-rank lattice L⊂ℍ and every ν∈ℕ03 with even |ν|≥2,

    Eν(L)=0.

    Corollary QC.4 · version 1

    Uses Construction QC.1, Proposition QC.2, Lemma QF.4.

    Earlier article. Current version; correspondence with the rewrite is unverified.

    Proof of Corollary QC.4

    By Lemma QF.4, Gν is odd when |ν| is even. The terms at λ and −λ cancel, and absolute convergence from Proposition QC.2 justifies pairing them.

    These are foundational identities, with no novelty claim. They explain how higher Eisenstein sums enter the regular part of the elliptic construction. Among the absolutely convergent sums, order 2 vanishes by Corollary QC.4, so order 3 is the first order not eliminated by this parity argument. For related lattice sums and Laurent coefficients, see [1], §§4–5.

    The full-lattice Eisenstein sum in Construction QC.1 is twice the positive-semilattice sum of [1, §4, eq. (8), p. 1018] at odd derivative order. The regularized first-derivative sum is [1, §4, eq. (12), p. 1019]. Proposition QC.3 uses real Taylor derivatives; the source’s expansion [1, eq. (13), p. 1020] uses Fueter polynomials.

    Eisenstein representations#

    Varying the lattice produces Eisenstein functions on SL₄. Their irreducible module, inducing parameter, and compact types are established there with proofs. This lattice action differs from the Möbius action on regular functions in the discrete-series and Hardy constructions.

    Harmonic theta series#

    The kernel derivatives have harmonic polynomial numerators. Their convergent lattice sums admit a Mellin formula; even integral lattices give harmonic theta cusp forms and critical L-values. The cubic example identifies a specific coefficient with the value at five of the weight-six newform η(2τ)¹².

    The order-one sum and its proposed connection to quasi-periods still need a regularization theorem. Other arithmetic lattices, Hecke decompositions, and algebraic period normalizations remain open directions.

    Open Directions#

    Multiplication and Arithmetic Lattices#

    Lattices with an action by elements of a quaternion algebra suggest transformation laws for the elliptic functions and quasi-periods. The action and its hypotheses need precise statements. A transformation identity and an independent arithmetic constraint are different conclusions.

    Period Relations#

    The additivity of ηL is part of Proposition QW.6. Relating it to a residue or to integrals on ℍ/L requires further statements fixing normalization, orientation, and left/right regularity. The closed pairings and pullback identities supply local results; quotient cohomology remains open.

    The literature guide collects sources and further reading. These questions remain in the research notebook for investigation.
