    Epstein Zeta Functions and Eisenstein Series on SL₄

    The Radial Epstein–Eisenstein Identity#

    Put Γ4=SL4(ℤ) and e4=(0,0,0,1). Let P3,1⊂SL4(ℝ) be the parabolic stabilizing the row line ℝe4. For real w>4, use the unnormalized radial section g↦‖e4g‖−w and sum it over (P3,1∩Γ4)\Γ4.

    Theorem EZ.1. [2, Lemma 2.9] For g∈SL4(ℝ) and real w>4,

    ∑m∈ℤ4∖{0}‖mg‖−w=2ζ(w)∑γ∈(P3,1∩Γ4)\Γ4‖e4γg‖−w.

    Both series converge absolutely.

    Theorem EZ.1 · version 1

    Uses Radial section conventions.

    This is the rank-four radial identity, with parameter s=w/4. The homogeneous-section construction, the quaternionic derivative sections and their normalized inducing character are treated in Theorem EM.3.

    General-Linear Induction and the Special-Linear Model#

    Gourevitch's classification concerns degenerate principal series of the general linear group [1, Thms. 1.1–1.2]. In its normalized (3,1) convention, the first character |det⁡A|w−2 and trivial second character restrict to the section law |d|−w on SL4(ℝ): the parabolic modular character restricts to |d|−4 and det⁡A=d−1.

    The moment map and special-linear harmonic transitions are treated in the lattice-Eisenstein article. Their explicit calculations and the coefficient-generated automorphic realization are proved there.
