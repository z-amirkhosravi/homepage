    Weak Hodge Decomposition and Cuspidal Cohomology

    The Poincaré-series construction has moved to the period-cocycle account.

    The flat-coefficient de Rham comparison identifies differential-form and group cohomology. The two results below supply the analytic comparison steps used for the explicit spinor two-form.

    Reduced Cohomology of a Hilbert Complex#

    Let (W∙,d) be a Hilbert complex: each dq is closed and densely defined, dq(dom⁡dq)⊆dom⁡dq+1, and dq+1dq=0 on dom⁡dq. Put Zq=ker⁡dq, Bq=im⁡dq−1 and ℌq=Zq∩(Bq)⟂, with orthogonality in Wq.

    Theorem 1. Weak Hodge Decomposition [7, §2.2, p. 5]

    For a Hilbert complex as above, the orthogonal decomposition is

    Wq=Bq⊕︎ℌq⊕︎(Zq)⟂.

    The inclusion of ℌq induces an isometric isomorphism with the reduced cohomology Zq/Bq.

    Theorem 1 · version 1

    Uses Hilbert complex conventions.

    The Hilbert-complex formulation originates with Brüning and Lesch; the displayed formulation is the one recalled in [7, §2.2]. The spinor-specific metric, norm constant and harmonicity calculation are Theorem 8 in “A Two-Form Construction for Regular Automorphic Spinors on the Quaternionic Ball”.

    Cuspidal-to-Ordinary Comparison#

    Theorem 2. Borel's Cuspidal Comparison [1, Cor. 5.5]

    Let 𝐆 be a Zariski-connected isotropic reductive group over ℚ with no nontrivial ℚ-rational characters. Write G=𝐆(ℝ), choose a maximal compact subgroup K, and let Γ be a torsion-free arithmetic subgroup. Let E be a finite-dimensional complex algebraic representation of 𝐆, and let ℰ be its associated local system on Γ\G/K. Write Lcusp2(Γ\G)∞ for the smooth cuspidal vectors, and H!q=im⁡(Hcq→Hq) for interior cohomology.

    The natural comparison

    Hq(𝔤,K;Lcusp2(Γ\G)∞⊗E)⟶Hq(Γ,E)

    is injective in every degree, and its image lies in H!q(Γ\G/K,ℰ).

    Theorem 2 · version 1

    Uses Arithmetic group and coefficient hypotheses.

    The verification for the explicit spinor form, its coefficient frame and the nonzero cuspidal class is Corollary 9 in “A Two-Form Construction for Regular Automorphic Spinors on the Quaternionic Ball”. The Poincaré-series construction is in Explicit Regular Cusp Forms and Period Cocycles for Sp(1,1).
