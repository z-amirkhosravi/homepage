    Exact Certificate for the Discriminant-Five Theta Kernel

    This supplement supplies the finite data in the arithmetic theta-kernel article. The infinite conclusions use harmonic theta modularity and the coefficient bounds used there. The calculations below use rational arithmetic, with explicitly extracted factors in ℚ(2,5) for the Euclidean Fueter numerators.

    The data file contains the integer/rational coefficient arrays. The standalone verifier, together with its polynomial operations, checks them using Python 3 and SymPy. Place these three files in one directory and run:

    python3 verify-certificate.py

    The verifier needs no network, PARI installation or project files. It writes its results to certificate-checks.json. Its checks are finite polynomial and lattice computations; the main article supplies their modular interpretation.

    Coordinate and Norm Conventions#

    The rational coordinates are a=t+xu+yv+zuv, with norm t2+2x2+5y2+10z2. The Euclidean coordinates used in Gν are (t,2x,5y,10z). Let SO have as columns the specified order basis in rational quaternion coordinates. Then

    SO=(101/21/2011/21/4001/20000−1/4).

    Order-coordinate vectors are columns. The order Gram matrix is AO=2SOtdiag⁡(1,2,5,10)SO. If SI is the ideal basis matrix in the main article, its normalized Gram matrix is AI=SItdiag⁡(1,2,5,10)SI. The missing factor two in the latter is exactly nI=2.

    The verifier checks multiplication closure of the order, right-ideal closure, the unimodular change from α𝒪 to the displayed ideal, both Gram determinants and levels, and 1∉I. The algebra is ramified at five and infinity: the Hilbert symbol (−2,−5)5=−1, while (−2,−5)2=1; other finite primes do not divide 2⋅2⋅5. The definite real place has symbol −1. Reduced discriminant five then proves maximality of the closed order.

    Isometries and the Invariant Basis#

    The following matrices generate the proper order isometries, in order coordinates:

    s1=(−10000−10000−10000−1).
    s2=(−1000001001001001).
    s3=(000−101000−1−10−1000).
    s4=(0−10−100100100−10−10).

    Conjugation in the same basis is

    CO=(10110−10000−10000−1).

    The first group has order 36; adjoining CO gives order 72. To prove exhaustiveness, each column z of an integral isometry must satisfy ztAOz=(AO)jj. Such vectors obey zi2≤(AO)jj(AO−1)ii. Enumeration in these finite boxes, followed by the pairwise Gram constraints, gives precisely the 72 generated matrices. Thus this checks the full group rather than only a subgroup.

    The harmonicity operator is ∑i,j(AO−1)ij∂i∂j; its scalar factor relative to the Euclidean Laplacian does not change its kernel. Appending the generator invariance equations gives rank 79 in the 84-dimensional sextic space. The displayed five columns are independent and solve all the equations. Appending conjugation gives rank 80. The corresponding degree-four calculation gives a one-dimensional proper-invariant space.

    Shell Sums and Mixed Numerator Span#

    The norm shells on the order, for N(a)=1,2,3,4,5, have sizes 6,18,24,42,6. In the basis above their first four sextic sum rows are

    (0000636000036−720−72−324721440144816).

    Rows one to three have rank three. The nullspace is generated by (0,0,1,0,0) and (0,−1,0,1,0). The four-coefficient bound makes both theta series zero. Conjugation splits this two-dimensional kernel into its even and odd lines.

    The mixed numerators are pulled back from I by q=αa, converted to order coordinates and averaged over the proper group. For each real component first divide by the common radical 2ϵ5δ, where

    ϵ=ν1+ν3+𝟏component i or k(mod2),δ=ν2+ν3+𝟏component j or k(mod2).

    Brandt and Averaging Checks#

    The data file records the three harmonic cubics, their Fischer Gram matrix, the nine compressed representation polynomials C(a), and B(1),…,B(5). The verifier reconstructs C(a) from the cubics and the average over the six units. It then checks the norm-shell matrices, the independence of all nine polynomial entries, and that the five commutant polynomials are proper-invariant. This proves the identification A↦tr⁡(AC(a)) with the complete invariant space.

    The even polynomial is normalized by P+=tr⁡(A+C)/7500, and the odd polynomial by P−=−tr⁡(A−C)/30000. Both their rational-coordinate and order-coordinate coefficient vectors are in the data file. The verifier checks these equalities and the two adjoint-parity identities.

    Finally it generates the three order-five numerators directly from Hν+ϵj=N∂jHν−2(|ν|+2)xjHν. After pullback to order coordinates, their weighted combination has common radical 5. The average over the enumerated 72-element group, after dividing by 5, is exactly −12000P+. Evaluation of the unaveraged numerator at (1,1,0,1) and division by 37 gives −1280/243.

    No numerical L-value approximation is used in these verifications. The bad-prime commutation argument and the deduction from zero theta series to absolutely convergent Eisenstein sums remain the infinite arguments given in the article.
